# Releases

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-29
- New: fourth tab „Wörter erkennen“ — five topics, each with an A1 and a B1 text where every word can be coloured by word type, subtype, case, sentence role or TeKaMoLo
- New: tap a word to see its word type, case (with Wer?/Wen?/Wem?/Wessen?), gender, sentence role, base form, meaning and why it has that form
- New: practice mode — colours off, guess the word type, its subtype and case, with a score and „Nächstes Wort“
- New: every word has its subtype — e.g. Possessivartikel, unterordnende Konjunktion, Modalverb, Relativpronomen, Wechselpräposition — with its own colour view „Unterart“
- New: the popup shows all forms of the word — article, pronoun and adjective tables, verb conjugation, the noun's eight case forms, or the whole group for prepositions, conjunctions and adverbs
- New: B1 texts show words as coloured rounded boxes, A1 texts as coloured text
- New: texts start plain — tap a word once to colour it, again to open it; „Alle färben“ and „Leeren“ colour or clear every word
- New: TeKaMoLo view — every adverbial marked Temporal, Kausal, Modal or Lokal
- New: „Satzbau“ on every text — each sentence split into subject, verb, objects and Te/Ka/Mo/Lo parts, with the word-order rules

## 1.0.2 — 2026-09-29
- New: nouns show their article and plural in the popup — die Wohnung · Pl. die Wohnungen — also for plural forms and compound words

## 1.0.1 — 2026-09-29
- New: tapping a word shows its translation right in the popup — meaning, other meanings, base form, and the whole sentence in English
- New: „Mehrere Wörter“ translates the chosen words together, with each word on its own

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-29
- New: one app for the three study pages — telc A1, telc B1 and Gesprächsthemen — switched from the top bar
- New: installable and offline, updates only when you press the version button
