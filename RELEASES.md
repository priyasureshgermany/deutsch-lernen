## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-29
- New: fourth tab „Wörter erkennen“ — five topics, each with an A1 and a B1 text where every word can be coloured by word type, subtype, case, sentence role or TeKaMoLo

- New: B1 texts show words as coloured rounded boxes, A1 texts as coloured text
- New: texts start plain — tap a word once to colour it, again to open it; „Alle färben“ and „Leeren“ colour or clear every word
- New: TeKaMoLo view — every adverbial marked Temporal, Kausal, Modal or Lokal
- New: „Satzbau“ on every text — each sentence split into subject, verb, objects and Te/Ka/Mo/Lo parts, with the word-order rules
